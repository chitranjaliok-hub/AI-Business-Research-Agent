# 🤖 AI Business Research Agent

## 🏆 Chettinad CodeFest 2026

An exploratory AI project developed during **Chettinad CodeFest 2026**, focused on using AI-assisted workflows to automate parts of the business research process.

This project was my introduction to **Agentic AI and AI agent workflows**, an area outside my core Data Science coursework. The project helped me explore how multiple AI-assisted components can work together to perform business discovery, information collection, and research summarization.

> **Project Status:** Exploratory / Learning Project

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

The objective was to develop an AI-assisted solution that could help automate business research by discovering businesses based on a user's query, collecting relevant information, and generating a structured research summary.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The project explored how an AI-based workflow could:

- Discover businesses based on a research query
- Collect publicly available business information
- Extract relevant details from collected information
- Organize the collected information
- Generate an AI-assisted research summary

---

## 🏗️ Project Workflow

The project was organized into multiple components:

### 1. Business Discovery

Identifies businesses relevant to the user's research query.

### 2. Business Detail Collection

Attempts to collect publicly available information about the discovered businesses.

### 3. Information Extraction

Processes the collected information and extracts useful business details.

### 4. Research Summary

Uses an AI model to generate a structured summary from the collected information.
